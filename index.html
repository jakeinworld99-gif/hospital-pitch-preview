Hermes Squad Preview

A seven-agent team that works inside your hospital's Slack.

Ask in plain English. The right agent picks it up, does the work, and writes back when it is done.

In pilot at one US hospital system.

How it works

Request, routed, done.

  1. 1
    You ask in Slack. A nurse, a coordinator, or a clinician types what they need into the channel they already use.
  2. 2
    The right agent picks it up. One of seven specialists — Nova, Atlas, Prime, Vera, Muse, Lens, Flux — claims the task based on what it is.
  3. 3
    The work gets done in Slack. You get a reply, a doc, or a ping when it is ready. No new tab. No new login.

Built for hospital reality

What we will and will not promise.

  • HIPAA-aware by default. Agents run on infrastructure configured for PHI. We sign BAAs. We do not train on your data.
  • Stays inside Slack. No separate app to install, no separate login, no shadow tool. Adoption follows the channel your team already lives in.
  • On-call during go-live. A real human responds inside 15 minutes between 7am and 9pm local, seven days a week, while the pilot runs.
  • No claim we cannot back. If a number on this page is not sourced, it is not here. The audit at the bottom of the page lists every claim.
